## Core Principles & Course Rules
1. **Course Scope (1MTR02 - TFC2, PUCP Mecatrónica 2026-2):**
   - Focus on complete engineering design, prototyping (physical or virtual), simulations, technical calculations, CAD/drawings across all domains, and experimental validation.
   - Adhere to engineering design standards: VDI 2221, VDI/VDE 2206, and Pahl & Beitz methodology.
2. **Academic Integrity & Quality Criteria:**
   - The final document ($DC$) must attain at least 13/20 to pass the course.
   - Maintain rigorous citations, technical justifications, and professional formatting in LaTeX.
3. **Semester Milestone Deliverables:**
   - **Avance 0 (Weeks 3-4):** Objectives (General/Specific), methodology, preliminary index, Gantt schedule.
   - **Avance 1 (Weeks 5-6):** Preliminary mechatronic design (geometries, preliminary sensor/actuator/controller selection, flowcharts, preliminary CAD/schematics).
   - **Avance 2 (Weeks 7-8):** Detailed engineering design, final component selection with technical calculations and justification.
   - **Avance 3 (Weeks 10-13):** Prototyping, virtual/physical simulations and validations, oral presentation prep.
   - **Documento Final & Feria (Weeks 14-15):** Complete technical report with financial/cost analysis, plans, and final oral defense / project fair.
